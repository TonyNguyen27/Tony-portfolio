## Post 1: Outbound pivot mistakes (2025)

**Link:** https://www.linkedin.com/posts/jasondbay_making-an-outbound-pivot-as-an-org-in-2025-activity-7307371379056631808-c0NW

**Date:** ~Mar 2025 (1y ago at collection)

**Topic:** Org-level outbound mistakes vs. what works

**Content (summary):**

Jason lists common failures when companies pivot to outbound: messaging written in silos (marketing, sales, or agencies alone), enablement from people who never did outbound, arbitrary activity targets, weak data/tech, no playbooks, leaders not making calls.

What works instead: collaborative messaging workshops with reps + marketing; standardized process with a library of winning emails/calls; lean stack (SEP + data + dialer); weekly enablement led by practitioners; managers "in the pit" with reps; outbound discussed in every 1:1.

**Key Insights:**

- Adoption rises when reps co-create messaging—they won't use what they didn't help build
- Scripts and recorded calls are assets, not optional nice-to-haves
- Executive and front-line leaders must model outbound behavior monthly

## Post 2: Cold email open rates & engagement metrics

**Link:** https://www.linkedin.com/posts/jasondbay_are-cold-email-open-rates-basically-a-useless-activity-7252650811451670529-hRLW

**Date:** ~2024 (6mo ago at collection)

**Topic:** Whether open rates still matter; how to track outbound properly

**Content:**

Jason argues open rates aren't useless but are overemphasized. Spam filters and tracking pixels distort the number—but multiple opens on one email is a real signal to invest more effort on that account.

Track outbound in three buckets:
- **Effort** — calls, emails, social touches
- **Efficiency** — pick-up %, open %, reply %
- **Effectiveness** — reply → meeting booked, stage progression

Related subject-line tactics (YouTube/webinars): ≤5 words, boringly obvious, avoid questions/punctuation—see `youtube-transcripts/jason-bay-cold-email-subject-lines.md`.

**Key Insights:**

- Opens are a signal, not the only metric—pair with replies and meetings booked
- High open count on one email = prioritize that prospect/account
- Don't optimize subject lines in isolation from body, CTA, and follow-up sequence
