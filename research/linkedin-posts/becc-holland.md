## Post 1: F-shaped emails are the worst cold emails

**Link:** https://www.linkedin.com/posts/beccholland-flipthescript_an-f-shaped-email-is-known-as-best-in-practice-activity-7128062148596957186-bCd7

**Date:** ~2024 (1y ago at collection)

**Topic:** Why "scannable" F-pattern emails fail in cold outreach

**Content:**

F-shaped reading (Nielsen Norman Group) is common when: (1) no formatting, (2) reader skims efficiently, (3) reader isn't interested enough to read every word. In cold email, F-pattern = lack of relevance—buyers take path of minimal effort.

**Key Insights:**

- "Best practice" blog formatting can signal low relevance to buyers
- Relevance + meaningful information beats bullet-heavy templates

## Post 2: 16-step prospecting sequence blueprint

**Link:** https://www.linkedin.com/posts/beccholland-flipthescript_for-most-of-my-prospecting-sequences-here-activity-6938519894162378753-fn-W

**Date:** ~2023

**Topic:** Multi-channel sequence design (Flip the Script)

**Content:**

Over 21 business days: 5 emails, 10 cold calls, 2 voicemails, 1 LinkedIn research step (pick 3 personalization angles). Personalization depth varies by touch—not every email is max research.

**Key Insights:**

- Outbound is a designed system across channels, not email-only
- CEO/founder who ran AE → VP sales before Flip the Script—practitioner-led frameworks
