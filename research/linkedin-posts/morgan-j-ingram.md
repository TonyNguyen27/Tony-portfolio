## Post 1: 52 failed videos before success

**Date:** ~Sep 2025 (8mo ago at collection)

**Topic:** Volume before automation in video prospecting

**Content:**

Morgan sent 52 prospecting videos that flopped; #53 got a response. Argues most breakthroughs happen between 70–100 attempts. Cites MrBeast's "100 videos" rule applied to LinkedIn outbound.

Challenge: 5 videos/day × 20 days = 100 manual touches before scaling with AI.

**Key Insights:**

- Video prospecting requires reps to find their voice through repetition
- "Show me your 100" is his coaching filter before discussing tools
