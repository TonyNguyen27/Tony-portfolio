## Post 1: Where top cold email operators converged (2024)

**Link:** https://www.linkedin.com/posts/alexanderberman_i-follow-every-cold-email-guru-in-the-space-activity-7264450005690662912-p-Dy

**Date:** ~2024 (1y ago at collection)

**Topic:** Modern cold email stack vs. outdated guru advice

**Content:**

Alex notes top practitioners now use: custom SMTP with very low volume per inbox; heavy lead-gen customization; Clay (or similar) to qualify before outreach—~5k leads/month at low cost.

Critiques outdated playbooks still teaching 30 emails/day, spintax, generic AI first lines from his 2021-era "Cold Email Manifesto" without updates.

**Key Insights:**

- Infrastructure and list quality matter as much as copy in 2024+
- Qualify leads before sending—don't spray unfit accounts
- Be skeptical of courses that haven't updated tactics in 3+ years

## Post 2: LinkedIn outreach as conversation

**Link:** https://www.linkedin.com/posts/alexanderberman_im-running-a-bunch-of-all-new-linkedin-outreach-activity-7268217132109225985-1rpZ

**Date:** ~2024

**Topic:** LinkedIn DM tactics (not email templates)

**Content (summary):**

Effective opener: simple question—"Hey Mike, are you still running cold emails for Acme?" Treat DMs like real-time conversation (voice notes), not long email-style pitches.

**Key Insights:**

- LinkedIn ≠ cold email channel—shorter, question-led, conversational
- Regulated industries may prefer LinkedIn where email is restricted
