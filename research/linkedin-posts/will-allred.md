## Post 1: How Lavender does email (prospecting stack)

**Link:** https://www.linkedin.com/posts/williamallred_how-lavender-does-email-activity-7054075756347338752-JEWA

**Date:** ~2023

**Topic:** Account-based outbound without spray-and-pray automation

**Content (summary):**

Will shares Lavender's prospecting approach: LinkedIn + Apollo for data, Lavender for coaching/drafting. Claims ~40% reply rate and ~28% meeting-booked rate with no-automation, account-based targeting—heavy research over volume.

**Key Insights:**

- Quality targeting beats inbox volume for Lavender's own GTM
- Email coaching in-flow (Lavender) improves rep craft, not just templates
- Co-founder practices the same outbound he sells to SDR teams
