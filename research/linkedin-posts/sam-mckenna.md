## Post 1: Why cold calls don't work (data-driven)

**Link:** https://www.linkedin.com/posts/samsalesli_samsales-smkym-samsales-activity-7364269293670211586-x3tA

**Date:** ~2025 (7mo ago at collection)

**Topic:** Modern buyer behavior vs. cold calling

**Content:**

Stats cited: ~11 dials to connect; ~1% cold calls → meetings; 37% show rate; 83% block unknown numbers; 90% of buyers pick vendors already on consideration lists (HBR). Advocates meeting buyers where they are—LinkedIn, SMKYM ("Show Me You Know Me"), researched outreach.

**Key Insights:**

- Cold call math rarely pencils for SaaS pipeline targets today
- Multi-touch LinkedIn + email with proof you know them beats blind dialing
- #samsales built pipeline on SMKYM methodology, not activity quotas alone

## Post 2: SMKYM email sequence framework

**Link:** https://www.linkedin.com/posts/samsalesli_samsales-semanticsmatter-linkedintips-activity-7113173501846413312-T0Dq

**Date:** ~2024

**Topic:** Three-email Show Me You Know Me sequence

**Content (summary):**

1. Subject includes "Show Me You Know Me" + authentic connection tied to research (no "hope this finds you well")
2. Follow-up within 48 hours with no new pitch
3. Third email: value on suspected challenge, no hard CTA—then personalized connection request (not InMail)

**Key Insights:**

- Semantics matter: say what you solve, not what you do
- Senior buyers first; avoid blank LinkedIn requests and profile-view stalking
