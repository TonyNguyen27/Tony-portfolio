## Post 1: Cold email that works (+ psychology)

**Link:** https://www.linkedin.com/posts/josh-braun_a-cold-email-that-works-plus-the-underlying-activity-7390452675496054784-uTZy

**Date:** ~Jan 2026 (4mo ago at collection)

**Topic:** Example cold email with psychological breakdown

**Content:**

Example subject: "Lainey Tick James, Half of the cold emails Lainey sends probably end up in spam..."

Body frames deliverability problem for Lainey's team, names ACME/BETA social proof, addresses objections (no fake inboxes, 10-min setup, fair price), CTA: "Worth a test?"

**Why it works (per Josh):** Relevance via teammate name; problem before pitch; preemptive objection handling ("labeling negatives defuses negatives"); low-risk CTA; human short sentences.

**Key Insights:**

- Products have no value without a named problem first
- "Worth a test?" lowers commitment vs. "book a demo"
- Tone should feel like a peer, not a marketer

**Content (summary):**
