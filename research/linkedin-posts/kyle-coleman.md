## Post 1: AI for account & contact intelligence

**Link:** https://www.linkedin.com/posts/kyletcoleman_sales-sdr-activity-7308580113351143424-gef3

**Date:** ~2025 (1y ago at collection)

**Topic:** AI as force multiplier for outbound research

**Content:**

AI should analyze: (1) executive priorities from press, interviews, trends; (2) contact intelligence beyond title—podcasts, LinkedIn posts, public appearances; (3) account-level playbooks.

Sellers use insights + human judgment in outreach—AI enhances touch, doesn't replace it.

**Key Insights:**

- "Where did they go to school" is not contact intelligence
- Codify research questions once, let AI synthesize account plans at scale
- Best teams train workflows on what good research looks like

## Post 2: New-year re-engagement emails

**Link:** https://www.linkedin.com/posts/kyletcoleman_sales-sdr-activity-7284631192421715969-26Tl

**Date:** ~Jan 2025

**Topic:** Follow-up quality vs. lazy "is now a good time?"

**Content:**

Bad: "You asked me to reach back out in the new year. Is now a better time for a call?"

Good: Reference prior initiative, share relevant use-case content, tie to what peer personas are prioritizing—earn the meeting with context.

**Key Insights:**

- Warm follow-ups need memory of the last conversation, not calendar triggers
- Provide value (content, insight) before asking for time again
