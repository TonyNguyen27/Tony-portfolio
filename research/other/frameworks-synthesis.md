# Cross-Expert Themes: B2B SaaS Cold Outreach

Synthesis from 10 practitioners (May 2026 collection). Not exhaustive—see individual files for sources.

## 1. Problem before product

**Josh Braun, Jason Bay, Becc Holland, Florin Tatulea** — Lead with a specific pain or trigger; product mention comes after the prospect sees themselves in the problem.

## 2. Subject lines and opens

**Jason Bay, Alex Berman** — Short (often ≤5 words), low-friction, avoid hype punctuation. Open rate gates everything else.

## 3. Show me you know me (research quality)

**Samantha McKenna, Morgan J. Ingram, Kyle Coleman, Will Allred** — Research must prove you understand the person/account (initiatives, posts, podcasts)—not mail-merge `{firstName}`.

## 4. Volume vs. craft

**Morgan J. Ingram, Josh Braun** — Manual repetition (e.g., 100 videos/messages) before automation; patterns emerge from doing the work.

**Alex Berman, Kyle Coleman** — Modern stack favors low sends per inbox + heavy qualification (Clay, custom SMTP), not 2019 spray volume.

## 5. Multi-channel sequences

**Becc Holland, Jason Bay, Samantha McKenna** — Email + LinkedIn + phone + voicemail as one designed system; most conversions after touch 5+.

## 6. AI as research amplifier

**Kyle Coleman, Will Allred (Lavender)** — AI for account/contact synthesis; humans write and send with judgment.

## 7. Org and enablement

**Jason Bay, Kyle Coleman** — Messaging workshops with reps; leaders make calls; playbooks from winning examples—not siloed marketing copy.

## 8. Offer and deliverability

**Patrick Dang, Alex Berman** — Weak offer or spam folder = copy cannot save you. Infrastructure and list fit matter.
